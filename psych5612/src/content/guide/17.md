## Ten practice tasks

1. Your lab partner believes in a supernatural realm but tests only natural hypotheses in the lab. Which naturalism describes the method, and which worldview need not follow?
2. Two patients have opposite patterns on naming and recognizing objects. Draw the evidence pattern and give one warranted inference and one unwarranted inference.
3. Explain why a culturally isolated inventive individual would not possess a whole civilization's technology.
4. A signal is 0.76 V with VIH = 0.70 V and guaranteed HIGH output VOH = 0.90 V. Explain how a buffer can help without changing the bit.
5. Fill all four rows of Y = (NOT A AND B) OR (A AND B). Simplify the function.
6. In the L08 traffic-light machine, start S0 with TA = 0; at the next two steps TB = 1. Trace states and outputs. Identify when an input is irrelevant.
7. A data input changes while the clock stays HIGH, well after the rising edge. Does the lecture's register immediately copy it? Explain.
8. Two TMs agree whenever both halt, but one halts on an additional input. Are they equivalent?
9. A UTM simulates a program for a million steps without halting. Does that establish the program never halts?
10. State the inference the systems reply challenges, then contrast it with the robot reply.

## Frequent mistakes to catch

- Saying dualism forbids interaction by definition. Interactionist dualism proposes it and owes an explanation.
- Equating naturalism with scientism, or methodology with an investigator's religion.
- Defining physicalism only as a denial that mental causation happens.
- Treating a double dissociation as complete modular independence.
- Giving Ayala the other reading's definition of culture.
- Claiming a chimp's interest in a book is automatically joint attention, or that chimps cannot cooperate at all.
- Calling a HIGH voltage exactly one fixed number or treating the forbidden interval as logical 0.
- Saying a buffer changes nothing physically because Y = A.
- Confusing tcd, the earliest possible output change, with tpd, the latest settling guarantee.
- Saying a register follows its input throughout HIGH, or that a common clock alone ensures correctness.
- Leaving out a register on every cycle or the setup/hold aperture.
- Saying any feedback loop is a well-behaved synchronous machine.
- Describing TM equivalence without the halting-domain condition.
- Confusing universal simulation with solving every mathematical problem.
- Treating equal computability as equal speed.
- Using “strong AI” to mean only broad task coverage instead of Searle's mental-state claim.
- Listing names of Chinese Room replies without their arguments.
- Memorizing only Q1-23 and skipping the readings without dedicated bank questions.
